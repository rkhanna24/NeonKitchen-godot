Formula (ADR 0004 §3): error=|actual-target|, max_error=max(target,5-target), penalty=weight×error, max_penalty=weight×max_error, score = 100 − ⌊(Σpenalty × 100) / Σmax_penalty⌋ — computed here with Math.floor(total*100/max), multiplication before division, exactly once, matching the game's integer arithmetic.
